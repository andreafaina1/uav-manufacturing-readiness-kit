# Detailed Assessment Questionnaire

Use this after the 20-question screen when a team wants a deeper manufacturing-readiness review.

## 1. Product & Prototype Maturity
- Is a production-intent architecture baseline defined and revision-controlled?
- Is the BOM complete enough to build the next batch without undocumented substitutions?
- Are requirements and acceptance criteria documented?
- Is the current prototype representative of the intended low-rate production configuration?
- Can the team identify exactly which configuration was built and tested for each unit?

## 2. Design for Manufacturing and Assembly
- Are parts easy to orient, access, fasten, inspect, remove and replace?
- Have unnecessary unique parts and variants been challenged?
- Are fastening methods, connectors, wiring practices and interfaces standardized where practical?
- Which steps depend on tacit expert judgment?
- Can modularity reduce assembly or repair time?

## 3. Assembly Flow & Takt
- Is the build sequence mapped from kitting through release?
- Are actual cycle times recorded for major steps?
- Are routing, queues, WIP and rework loops visible?
- Is demand converted into takt using realistic productive time?
- Are labor content and station capacity compared with target rate?

## 4. Quality & Inspection Readiness
- Are critical-to-quality characteristics identified?
- Are acceptance criteria unambiguous?
- Are incoming, in-process and final inspection points defined?
- Are test procedures repeatable and version-controlled?
- Is there one defect/nonconformance log linked to units/builds?
- Is corrective action closed with an effectiveness check?

## 5. Supply Chain & Make/Buy
- Are critical, long-lead and single-source parts mapped?
- Are supplier lead times and capacity assumptions evidenced?
- Are critical suppliers qualified using fit-for-purpose evidence?
- Are substitution or redesign options defined for fragile items?
- Is make/buy based on capability, quality, capacity, lead time and resilience?

## 6. Tooling, Fixtures & Test Equipment
- Is there a controlled tool/equipment list by build step?
- Are measurement and test devices verified/calibrated as appropriate?
- Which steps need alignment, holding, routing or other fixtures for repeatability?
- Is production test equipment separated from prototype-only lab setups where needed?
- Is a minimum viable production cell defined for the next target rate?

## 7. Documentation & Standard Work
- Are work instructions available for the repeatable build sequence?
- Do instructions use visual standards where useful?
- Does each unit have a build record/traveler?
- Are drawing/BOM/software/process changes controlled?
- Are training needs defined per task?
- Could a new technician build with limited coaching?

## 8. Field Repairability & Sustainment
- Are recurrent failure or damage-prone modules identified from evidence?
- Are replaceable modules designed and documented for practical access?
- Are required tools and spares understood?
- Is repair-vs-replace logic defined?
- Are repair/failure records fed back into engineering and manufacturing priorities?

## 9. Scale-up Path
- Are 10-, 50- and 100-unit scenarios expressed as rate, staffing, tooling, space and supplier assumptions?
- Has the team written down what is expected to break at each scale step?
- Is hiring/onboarding/cross-training capacity planned?
- Have critical suppliers confirmed next-rate capacity assumptions?
- Is there a weekly ramp review using rate, quality, shortages, rework and action closure?
